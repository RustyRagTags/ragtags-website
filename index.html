Combat & raid rules

Defend yourself. Retaliate if you need to. Just don’t pretend you started nothing.

Self-defence

  • If another player damages you first, you and your team get a 10-minute self-defence window.
  • Kills in valid self-defence cost 0 reputation.
  • Self-defence extends to the attacker’s current team.

Raid defence

  • If another team damages your base, you can defend with 0 reputation penalty.
  • This defence window remains active during the raid and for 15 minutes after the last structure hit.
  • Raiders become Wanted for 6 hours.

Retaliation

  • If another team raids you, you get a 24-hour retaliation window.
  • A retaliation raid has a reduced start penalty of -5 reputation instead of -18.
  • Retaliation only applies against the original aggressor’s team.

Daily recovery

  • You must be online peacefully for at least 30 minutes to earn recovery.
  • No passive offline regeneration.
  • Recovery bands: +5 / +4 / +3 / +2 / +1.

How the wipe progresses

Days 1–7

Week 1 protection

After 10 minutes offline, bases are hard protected. This gives casual teams time to establish themselves.

Day 8 onward

Reputation phase

Offline raiding is enabled again. Defence strength, gather and upkeep now depend on the team’s reputation.

All modifiers return to normal. Raid freely, fight freely, and blow through the end of wipe without restrictions.

FAQ

Quick answers

Do personal blueprints wipe every month?

No. Personal BPs are retained. The map wipes monthly on the official Rust monthly schedule.

How is team reputation calculated?

By the lowest current member in the team. One hostile player can drag the whole team into a worse band.

Can I defend myself without being punished?

Yes. Valid self-defence kills cost 0 reputation. Raid defence is also protected, including 15 minutes after the last hostile structure hit.

When exactly does Purge happen?

Purge begins automatically 24 hours before the official monthly wipe and ends when the map wipe occurs.
